Know a link is a scam before you click it

Yaqiz is a Chrome extension that checks Google Search results, Google Maps places and the sites you open, and explains every warning in English or Arabic.

Code on GitHub ☕ Buy me a coffee
Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store.

Google Search

A green, yellow or red badge next to every result, with the reason.

Google Maps

Warnings on fake listings, such as "pay your fines" with a personal mobile number.

Any website

High-risk sites are blocked before they load; dangerous downloads are paused.

🚩 User reports

Report a site or number. Every report is reviewed; approved ones protect every user.

How it works

Checks run on your device. The extension combines several signals into a 0–100 score: does the link impersonate a known organisation, is the domain new, who owns the IP, does the page ask for an OTP or card details on an unofficial site, and has it been reported and confirmed?

Your browsing history is never sent to any server. See the privacy policy.
